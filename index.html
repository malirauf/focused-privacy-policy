Privacy Policy

Focused — Block Distractions

Last updated: July 14, 2026


This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

Focused is designed to operate entirely on your device. We do not operate backend servers, require user accounts, display advertisements, or collect analytics.

Information the App Accesses

Location (Foreground & Background)

Focused uses your device's location to detect when you enter or leave the Focus Zones you create (for example, your office, home, library, or gym).

  • Location is used only for geofence monitoring.
  • Zone coordinates and radius are stored locally on your device.
  • Background location enables automatic zone detection when the app isn't open.
  • Your location is never transmitted to servers operated by us.

Installed Applications

Focused accesses the list of installed applications on your device so you can choose which apps to block inside your Focus Zones.

This information never leaves your device.

Accessibility Service

Focused uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to determine which application is currently in the foreground in order to enforce your selected app blocking rules.

  • Accessibility data is processed entirely on your device.
  • We do not read messages, passwords, typed text, or screen content.
  • Accessibility data is never collected, stored, or transmitted by us.

Notifications

Focused may request notification permission to notify you when Focus Zones become active or when a blocked application is intercepted.

Data Storage

All app data, including:

  • Focus Zones
  • Blocked application lists
  • Preferences

is stored locally on your device using Room and Android DataStore.

We do not operate servers that store your personal information.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, locally stored app data may be included in your personal Google account backup, subject to Google's backup policies.

Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data from your device.

Third-Party Services

Focused relies on the following Google services to provide core functionality:

  • Google Play Services (Location & Geofencing)
  • Google Maps SDK

These services are used solely to provide mapping and geofence functionality.

Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Focused does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your Choices

  • You can revoke Location permission at any time.
  • You can disable Accessibility permission at any time.
  • You can disable Notifications whenever you choose.
  • You can delete Focus Zones from within the App.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.

Intended Audience

Focused is intended for users aged 18 years or older and is not designed for children.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
